# AsistentiAI: AI chatbot and agent for customer messages in Albanian

**AsistentiAI** ([asistentiai.com](https://asistentiai.com/en)) is an Albanian platform that gives any business an AI customer-service agent. The agent answers customers 24/7, within seconds, on the website chat, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp. It replies in Albanian, even when customers write without ë and ç or in dialect, and also in English, Italian or the customer's own language. It uses only the business's own knowledge and never makes up prices, stock or policies. When it isn't sure, it hands the conversation to the team with a ready draft reply. Every channel lands in one shared team inbox. Plans start at $120 per month.

## How it works

1. **It learns your business.** You give it your website address, and the agent reads it on its own. It also learns from documents (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV), product lists, a photo of your price list, the information you add in the dashboard and WooCommerce products, which update automatically. If something is missing, it asks you instead of guessing.
2. **It answers 24/7.** Customers get replies within seconds, with your prices, hours and rules. The agent understands the context of a conversation and recognizes products from customers' photos.
3. **It doesn't invent.** It never makes up prices, stock or policies. When it doesn't know or isn't sure, it says so honestly.
4. **It hands over to a person.** The conversation is marked "Needs you" in the dashboard, with a short summary and a ready draft reply for a colleague to check and send. The team can take over any conversation at any time, even from a phone.
5. **It learns from your team.** It learns from colleagues' answers for next time. Questions it didn't know show up in the dashboard for the business to fill in.

### The shared team inbox

- Website, Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp in one real-time inbox, also on mobile.
- Four roles: Administrator, Supervisor, Operator and Staff/Editor. Each person has their own permissions.
- Sign-in protected with two-step verification or passkeys.
- Conversation routing, team hours and automatic closing.
- Labels, filters, saved views, quick replies, internal notes and search within a conversation.
- Orders and bookings straight from the chat, and tasks with deadlines for colleagues.
- Shows which Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp ad the customer came from.
- Analytics: reply time per channel (agent vs colleagues) and each colleague's work. Reports download as Excel, CSV or Word, and a weekly report arrives by email.

### Bookings, leads and orders

- The agent records bookings and leads (name, contact, interest) from the chat. Appointment businesses also get a calendar, an online booking page and reminders.
- When a WooCommerce store or your own system is connected (an ERP, via the [Connector Spec](docs/connector-spec.md)), the agent checks live order status and stock, but only after it has verified the customer.
- When a product is out of stock, the agent can offer a call from the team when it's back. The team gets a task when the product returns.
- Optional: the agent can know about the offers the business posts on its own Facebook page and Instagram, including the price, any gifts and the end date. This is off until the business turns it on.

### Control and data

- You can switch the agent on or off, give it a schedule, or pause it on a single channel.
- Each business has its own isolated space. The database is in the EU (Frankfurt).
- Data is never sold, and it isn't used for advertising or to train general models. See the [privacy policy](https://asistentiai.com/en/privacy).
