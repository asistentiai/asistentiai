# Adding the AsistentiAI chat to your website

The website chat (widget) is one of AsistentiAI's channels, next to Instagram, Messenger and WhatsApp. Every plan includes it. On your website, the same AI agent answers visitors 24/7, within seconds, in Albanian, English, Italian or the visitor's own language. Its conversations land in the same team inbox as your other channels.

## What you need

- **An AsistentiAI account.** There is no self sign-up. You [request a free trial](https://asistentiai.com/en/signup) (50 agent replies, no credit card), and our team opens the account within the day.
- **Access to your website's HTML.** You need to be able to add one line of code to your site.

## Steps

1. **Give the agent your website address.** The agent reads the site on its own and learns what you offer, your contacts and your hours.
2. **Fill in what's missing.** Anything that isn't on the site, you're asked to write. Nothing is guessed.
3. **Add the widget.** Your website's embed code is one line, and you get it from the AsistentiAI dashboard. Paste it into your website's HTML. Your messaging channels (Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp) can be connected whenever you want them.
4. **Supervise from the dashboard.** You can see every conversation, step in whenever you want and learn what customers ask.

> Copy the embed code from your own dashboard. It is specific to your business, so don't copy it from another site or from an example.

If your site builder or CMS lets you add custom HTML to a site-wide area, such as the footer or a theme setting, putting the line there makes the chat appear on every page.

## What visitors get

- Answers from your own information: your website, documents, product list or catalog, and what you add in the dashboard. The agent never makes up prices, stock or policies.
- Product recognition: a visitor can send a photo, and the agent recognizes products by comparing it with your catalog photos.
- A person when needed. When the agent doesn't know or isn't sure, it says so honestly and hands the conversation to your team, marked "Needs you" in the dashboard, with a ready draft reply. Your team can take over any conversation at any time.

## Control

- You can switch the agent on or off, give it a schedule, or pause it on a single channel, for example only on the website chat.
- Each business has its own isolated space, and data is never sold or used for advertising. See the [privacy policy](https://asistentiai.com/en/privacy).

## No website?

A website isn't required. The agent also works with just Instagram, Messenger or WhatsApp. If you'd like a professional website or online shop, AsistentiAI can build one for you. It is connected to the agent from day one, so the agent picks up your products, prices and orders by itself. Pricing is by quote, based on your needs ([request a quote](https://asistentiai.com/en/contact?tema=web)).
